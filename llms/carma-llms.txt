# Carma

> Carma is an Australian online used-car retailer for buying, selling, and trading in inspected, reconditioned pre-owned vehicles online, with bundled financing, home delivery, a 7-day money-back guarantee, a 3-month warranty, and NRMA roadside assistance. Backed by General Catalyst; headquartered in Sydney, Australia. Carma publishes no public developer API at this time.
